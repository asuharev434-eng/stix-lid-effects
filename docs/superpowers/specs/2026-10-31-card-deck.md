# Card deck effect

Approved design: 52 real playing-card faces with ranks/suits; card backs are corresponding desktop fragments. The initial tiled backs reproduce the desktop. Cards lift, adopt poker proportions, turn in 3D and fly outward toward the viewer. Restoration reverses the same scene into the current desktop. Add one selector entry and keep Option+M/pinch routing and input shielding.

Use deterministic cached rectangle geometry and a CoreGraphics/CoreText face atlas drawn locally. No external art, image generation dependency or runtime network. Reuse the glass vertex ABI and depth attachment, but give cards their own shader pipeline. True homogeneous perspective clips cards as they pass the camera. Initial progress zero must match the desktop; progress one must be entirely black. Cards stop rendering during held black.

Affected files: CardDeckMesh.swift, CardArtwork.swift, EffectStyle.swift, DepthRenderer.swift, DepthShaders.swift, ManualEffectController.swift and GPU/geometry tests. Risks: card face orientation, texture atlas bleeding, near-plane artifacts and mesh cache switching. Validate a synthetic rendered frame sequence before installing the signed build. Preserve current camera and input permissions.

Verification: 39 tests pass, including exact clear-screen coverage, all card IDs, middle-flight visibility, opaque black endpoints and runtime shader compilation. Exported and visually inspected synthetic face atlas and GPU frames. Signed 0.7.0 installed on the target Mac; the selector includes cards and logs confirm a complete close/held-black/reverse/clear cycle for style 9. Camera gestures were briefly paused to isolate the test and are restored afterward.
